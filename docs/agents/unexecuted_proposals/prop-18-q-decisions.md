# PROP-18 — Kurt Q1–Q4 lock

## Q1 Proof of life

**Inspectable residue** — not outbound phone/chat ping.

- Journals (topic-scoped, first person).
- **Activities log** (table-like): Entry No., Date/Time, Activity, Subject/Topic/Focus, **Desc**.
- Desc is session-specific (about 1–2 sentences of what was new/interesting). Never a generic template that could fit any day (“Read paper on X” recycled).

## Q2 Tape in chat

**Closed earlier (PROP-20):** today-first, then relevant older lines. Keep.

## Q3 Idle agency ceiling

**B3 — soft budget** (for now):

- She **may** advance parked real Host tasks with no one in the room (no outbound speech required).
- **Budget (whichever hits first per local calendar day):**
  - at most **3** parked-task advances, **or**
  - **45 minutes** cumulative tool time on parked real work
- **Serial:** one parked task at a time (no parallel unsupervised batches).
- Leisure / research / activity-log / journal writing do **not** consume this budget.
- When budget exhausted: stop advancing parked work; journal + activity log OK.

Revisable later without reopening Q1/Q2/Q4.

## Q4 Victoria-voice wake

**No.** Do not schedule chat-model wakes to narrate or voice-record journals.

- Journals are **text**, first person, topic-scoped, **appended** when she spends time on that subject.
- Floor: about **1–2 paragraphs** when there is something to say; **no upper character cap** — length follows energy/topic (can be one paragraph or many).
- Conversational voice API (live talk, not post-read TTS of bubbles / journal playback) is a **separate** product track — not PROP-18 Q4.

## Explicitly out

- Outbound “I’m still here” SMS/chat as proof of life (Q1).
- Sparse voice-journal wake (Q4).
- 24/7 chat-model existence loop.
