# TASK-156 Report — Best-effort SpeakAsync after chat.done

**Overall: PASS**

## Problem (from QA-130 AC7)

After a successful `chat.done`, `SpeakAsync` was never reached because `RequestAborted` cancelled at `_emotion.GetAsync` (line ~500) during post-chat side effects. UE being down is separate — speak must still be *attempted*.

## Fix

In `SoulCore.Host/Ws/ChatWebSocketHandler.cs` post-reply Unreal path:

1. Use `CancellationToken.None` (`sideEffectCt`) for emotion snapshot, `SetEmotionAsync`, `SpeakAsync`, loco, animation, and look after `chat.done`.
2. Do **not** share the dying WS / `RequestAborted` CT for those side effects.
3. Log speak outcome distinctly:
   - `Post-chat SpeakAsync succeeded (replyLen=…)`
   - `Post-chat SpeakAsync attempted — Unreal not connected (no-op)`
   - `Post-chat SpeakAsync returned false (connected but speak failed)`
   - `Post-chat SpeakAsync/emotion side-effect cancelled` (`OperationCanceledException`)

**Not changed:** tool-loop / `recall_memory`, Strategy A keyword fallback, episodic authoring CT (still request-scoped; failures remain best-effort with existing catch).
